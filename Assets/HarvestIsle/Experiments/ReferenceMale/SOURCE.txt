This is an adapted CC0 base, not a claim of an entirely original hand-sculpted mesh.
Motion study: 49-bone weighted rig adapted from MakeHuman default.mhskel
and default_weights.mhw (CC0; preserved with the source data).
Tools/build_reference_male_rig.py propagates skin weights through subdivision,
normalizes four influences per vertex and exports ReferenceMaleSkin.bytes.
Unity creates native skinned meshes and procedural motion in ReferenceMaleMotion.
11 in-place study actions; no gameplay integration or mobile optimization.

Outfit: fitted green short-sleeve T-shirt and blue full-length trousers.
Tools/reference_male_clothes.py derives separate garment panels from the CC0
body surface, adds ease and folded opening trims, and transfers skin weights.
Covered body triangles are omitted from the clothed renderer to prevent poke-through.
No cloth physics; the outfit deforms with the same skeleton as the body.
Garment generation statistics: clothes-report.json. Clothed total: rig-report.json.
